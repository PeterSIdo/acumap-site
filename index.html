Four systems in one place

Scalp and ear points can be reached easily with the patient seated and fully clothed, which makes them ideal for the elderly and anyone who finds lying down difficult.

YNSA

Yamamoto New Scalp Acupuncture

Basic, Sensory, Brain and Y-Points on lateral, frontal and posterior views, with neck and abdomen diagnosis areas linked to each meridian.

TCM

Chinese Scalp Acupuncture

Standard scalp areas, motor area measurement, meridians on the head, organ areas and brain zones.

ZSA

Zhu's Scalp Acupuncture

The treatment zones of Zhu's system, with their locations and indications.

Auriculotherapy

Ear Acupuncture

Chinese auriculotherapy, musculoskeletal, internal organ and neuroendocrine points, plus descriptions and treatment formulas.

About the author

AcuMap was created by Peter, an acupuncture therapist at Lothian Massage & Acupuncture in East Lothian, Scotland. Years in clinic showed how valuable a quick reference is when choosing points or building a treatment plan, and how well scalp and ear acupuncture work alongside traditional Chinese acupuncture. AcuMap grew out of that experience.

It was made with love for acupuncture and with respect for every practitioner, renowned or unknown, who has helped develop this wonderful system of healing. Where our text is based on a published work, the source is credited alongside it.
